RockPower

Rooted Upon The Rock Solar Installation | Soweto. Cut costs. Create jobs. Keep power.

Load shedding and generator costs hurt township small businesses and leave young people without safe, powered places to meet. RockPower helps both, in one app.

What you can do

  1. AuditSee in 60 seconds what your business loses each month.
  2. QuoteGet a solar system size and price, and share it on WhatsApp.
  3. Kits & JobsApply for a pay-as-you-save kit and hire a local young installer.
  4. PowerBook solar hub power for your business or youth event and earn green points.
  5. ImpactSee the savings and jobs the app is creating.
